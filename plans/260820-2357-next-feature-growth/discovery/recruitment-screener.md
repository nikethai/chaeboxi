# Recruitment screener

**Target:** 8–12 qualified participants  
**Segment:** multi-model power users  
**Primary metric later:** weekly retention (not measured here)

## Qualify (all required)

1. Uses at least two of: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, a local model, or a BYOK client, in a typical week.
2. Has at least one project that spans multiple sessions or days (not one-shot Q&A).
3. Can legally provide a real conversation export **or** complete the observed copy/paste + handoff tasks with their own history on-screen.
4. Adult; not sharing an employer export they are not allowed to remove.

## Mix (aim)

| Bucket | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Current Chaeboxi or similar BYOK-client power user | 3–4 |
| Abandoned multi-model (went back to one provider) | 2–3 |
| Manual notes / paste-into-docs workflow | 2–3 |

## Disqualify

- Only uses one provider and has no multi-session project
- Wants a hosted Chaeboxi account / cloud sync of vendor archives
- Cannot complete a 45-minute session
- Team/Enterprise workspace export they do not own

## Script (5 minutes)

- "Which AI products did you use in the last 7 days?"
- "Tell me about a project that continued across more than one chat."
- "Last time you switched models mid-project, what did you copy?"
- "Would you download your ChatGPT or Claude data export for this study if we never upload it?"
- Record: weekly continuity yes/no, export willingness yes/no/maybe, notes vs archive.

Do not promise a shipping Continuity feature.
