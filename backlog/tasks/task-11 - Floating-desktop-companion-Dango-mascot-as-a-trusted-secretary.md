## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Extend the existing Dango mascot into an optional floating desktop pet, inspired by the Codex pet: bounce or wander around the desktop and offer speech-bubble suggestions or important connected-account notifications such as incoming email. This extends the accepted redesign mascot and UX flow 21 / MB1 menu-bar mascot and notification rules rather than introducing a second mascot. It is the friendly face of Dango as an AI home base and trusted secretary, not an attention-demanding toy. Explore quiet hours, snooze/hide controls, and permission-scoped nudges. Idea only; implementation is not scheduled.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Users can opt into a floating desktop mascot and hide or snooze it.
- [ ] #2 Speech-bubble nudges respect quiet hours and use only explicitly authorized sources.
- [ ] #3 A relevant bubble can open the underlying conversation or source item.
